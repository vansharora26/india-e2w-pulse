Checks on the latest refresh · —

    Data. Registrations from the Vahan dashboard (vahan.parivahan.gov.in), April 2021 to date. The current month is shown to date and left out of comparisons. Vahan keeps adding late registrations, so recent months can rise slightly on the next refresh.

    Definitions

    e2W: Vahan vehicle categories Two Wheeler (NT), Two Wheeler (T) and Two Wheeler (Invalid Carriage) with fuel ELECTRIC(BOV) or PURE EV. Low-speed models under 25 km/h need no registration and are not counted. Makers are grouped into brands; all other makers are in Others. Penetration: e2W registrations divided by all two-wheeler registrations in the same months. 1 lakh = 100,000. FY runs April to March, so FY26 is April 2025 to March 2026.
